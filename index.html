Our story

Northern tailoring, cut for the modern man.

BMY Collection sits at the heart of Gombe, dressing men for the mosque, the wedding hall and the office alike. Our atelier pairs the region's textile heritage — shadda, brocade, guinea — with silhouettes built for how our clients actually live.

Every bespoke piece begins with a conversation, not a size chart. Every ready-to-wear piece is finished to the same standard we hold for a custom order, because a shelf should never mean a shortcut.

How bespoke works

From first measure to first wear.

A bespoke order at BMY Collection moves through six stages, each one done by hand in our Gombe workroom.

Consultation

Tell us the occasion, silhouette and fabric you have in mind — in person or via WhatsApp.

Measurement

A full hand-measure at the atelier, recorded to your personal pattern for future orders.

Fabric Selection

Choose from shadda, yards, brocade and imported cloth, or bring your own material.

Cutting

Your pattern is cut by hand against your measurements — nothing is mass-produced.

Stitching & Fitting

The piece is assembled, embroidered where required, and fitted on your body before finishing.

Delivery

Collect in-store or have your finished piece delivered within Gombe and beyond.
