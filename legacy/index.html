The Atelier Story

Where heritage meets the everyday.

A caftan is not simply worn. It is a record of where you come from, cut fresh for how you live now.

BMY Collection works from the heart of Gombe, pairing the North's textile heritage — shadda, guinea brocade, atiku — with silhouettes built for the mosque, the wedding hall, and the office alike. Every bespoke piece begins with a conversation, not a size chart; every ready-to-wear piece is finished to the same standard we hold for a custom order.

Services & Process

Three ways to order.

Whether it's one piece or an entourage, every order starts with a conversation at the atelier.

№ 01

Bespoke

4–6 weeks · 2–3 fittings

A one-of-one garment, built from your measurements. Fabric, embroidery and silhouette are entirely your call — caftan, agbada or full ensemble.

Price on request
№ 02

Ready-to-Wear

Available now · No fitting required

Finished caftans, vests and trousers in a range of sizes and colourways — the same standard of finish, ready to take home today.

Price on request
№ 03

Group & Family Orders

6–8 weeks · Coordinated fittings

One fabric, unified across grooms, groomsmen and family — shadda and yards sourced and cut as a matching set for the occasion.
